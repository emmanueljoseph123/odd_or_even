How To Play

1. The Toss Call ODD or EVEN, then choose a finger number (1 to 6). If the sum of your number and the computer's number matches your call, you win the toss!
2. Scoring Runs (Batting) Tap any finger count from 1 to 6. If your number is different from the bowler's, you add that many runs to your score!
3. Getting OUT If both you and the computer throw the SAME number on the same turn, the batter is OUT!
4. Winning the Match Each match lasts up to 5 rounds (or until a wicket falls). The team chasing in the second innings must beat the target score to win!
